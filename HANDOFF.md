# CCC Handoff

Project state, the decisions behind it, and what is still open. Read this before
starting a session on anything CCC. It is the memory of the project, so add to it
rather than rewriting it.

---

## The engagement

Chris Maier came on board in August 2026. CCC is a national business forum that
runs invitation only events for the commercial real estate industry across twenty
chapters. The site is strong. The outbound email is Times New Roman on white with
a signature block, which is the entire gap this work closes.

**Chris runs the chapters nationally.** Atlanta is his home chapter and the URL he
gives out, but most sends go to other markets. Nothing in the templates should
read as an Atlanta organisation with satellite offices. The chapter list is a
credential and belongs in the footer; individual sends name their own market
through `{{CHAPTER_CITY}}`.

## What shipped, 19 August 2026

**Brand package** (`brand/ccc-brand-package.html`). Palette, type, logo rules,
components, voice, signature, email build rules, and an index of the suite.
Colour values were sampled out of the logo PNG rather than eyeballed. Components
are the ones the live site already uses.

**Quarterly newsletter** (`templates/newsletter/`). Nine sections: founder letter,
next event, quarter in review with a numbers strip and three recap cards, dark
speaker grid, chapter report, member spotlights, sponsor tiers, three ways to get
involved, full footer.

**Five correspondence templates** (`templates/correspondence/`). Logo band, logo
band plus hero, plain letter, hero first, and split. They exist because the
register has to shift between a mass confirmation and a one to one note without
the brand shifting with it. Split is the newest: logo on one line, body and a
portrait image side by side, then the signature.

**A filled preview of every template**, generated by `templates/fill-mock.mjs`.
Each pair sits together in the same folder, `x-template.html` next to
`x-preview.html`. The template is the master and the preview is derived, so a
layout change plus a re-run keeps the two in step. Hand written duplicates would
have drifted inside a week. A preview edited by hand is lost on the next run.

**Six event templates** (`templates/events/`). Chapter invitation, summit
invitation with priced tiers, speaker lineup, final call, recap with photo grid,
sponsor partners.

**One repo, everything in it.** `github.com/nolanbowlin/ccc-chris-maier`, public, GitHub Pages enabled. Images serve from `https://nolanbowlin.github.io/ccc-chris-maier/assets/img/`. There was briefly a second repo just for images, which existed only because this one was private at the time. Both are public now, so the split had no reason to exist.

**Reference capture.** `reference/ccc-homepage-member-area-2026-08-19.pdf`. The
original was a 17MB image only print of the member area. Re-encoded at 1000px
wide, quality 60, down to 1.17MB, a 93 percent reduction, still fully legible.

## Decisions worth not relitigating

**One accent colour.** CCC orange `#EE7700` and CCC charcoal `#333333`, and that
is the whole palette. Depth comes from dark panels, not more hues. Every sponsor
logo in a CCC email brings its own colour already.

**Heading type is a system stack, not a webfont.** The site loads Allerta from
Google Fonts. The templates list `'Allerta','Segoe UI',Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif`
with an `@import` so Apple Mail and iOS render the real face, while Outlook and
Gmail fall back cleanly. Relying on the webfont alone would silently drop those
clients back to a default serif, which is the current problem.

**Title frozen at National President &amp; Founder.** Chris's own sends drift:
July used "President &amp; Founder" with thirteen chapters, the later ones use
"National President &amp; Founder" with twenty. The templates use the longer,
more recent version everywhere. If Chris prefers the shorter one, change it in
all eleven files at once, not gradually.

**Signature URL is the national root**, `www.contractorsclosersconnections.com`,
not the Atlanta subdomain. Chapter specific sends can override it.

**Luma is not being replaced, its email is.** Luma is a reasonable ticketing back
end. What it produces today is a Luma email with a CCC logo dropped into it.
`evt-02-invite-summit.html` is a CCC email whose button points at whatever
checkout exists, Luma included.

**Photography stays out of the email at full resolution.** Recap sends are the
one place this gets violated in practice. Gmail clips a message over roughly
102KB of HTML and hides the tail behind a "view entire message" link, which is
exactly where the next event date sits. Grid photos are 560x400 crops, heroes are
1200x480.

**Centre crop, never attention crop.** The first pass ran sharp's `attention`
crop, which finds the highest contrast face and zooms to it. On a 2.5:1 hero that
throws the room away: the recap hero came out as the back of one head and half of
another at a bar. Centre crop trims top and bottom only and keeps the scene. If
new photography is added, use `position:'centre'` and check the result.

**The mock set uses real CCC content, not invented content.** Confluence, the AI
Revolution, the Build to Rent Summit, the speaker roster and both sponsor tiers
are all lifted from Chris's own sends and the live site. The only invented values
are numbers CCC has not published: attendance, ticket prices, seats remaining and
the quarterly stat strip. Those are marked `INVENTED` in `fill-mock.mjs` and
called out in the gallery. Keep that split if you extend the mocks, because the
approval copy is the one document Chris reads closely.

**Placeholders ship inside the templates on purpose.** The speaker, member and
sponsor slots carry charcoal fields with the C mark rather than nothing, so a
template opens looking finished and shows its real crop ratios before the actual
images arrive.

## Session log

**2026-08-19.** Folder created. Brand package, quarterly, four correspondence and
six event templates built. Logo variants derived, event photography cropped, three
Chris portraits processed. Member area PDF compressed 17MB to 1.17MB. Two GitHub
Pages asset repos created and verified, one for CCC and one for Peachtree
Planning. All template asset URLs return 200. Tag balance checked across all
twelve HTML files. Largest template is 33KB of HTML, well inside Gmail's 102KB
clipping threshold.

Later the same day: two more event frames arrived, both with the CCC banner
legible, which is where the tagline above came from. Added as heroes, grid photos
and a portrait crop of Chris at the microphone. The uncompressed 17MB member area
PDF and all original photographs were moved into `source/` so the folder root
holds only the two markdown files and the four working directories.

Then a review pass against live renders, which caught what structural checks could
not:

- Long `{{TOKENS}}` have no spaces, so they cannot wrap and they forced the fixed
  600px table wider than 600px. Only a preview problem, tokens are gone in a real
  send, but every template looked broken. Fixed with `td{word-break:break-word}`
  and `table-layout:fixed` on the container.
- Every hero and grid photo was recropped, see the centre crop note above.
- Heroes were reassigned so the strong frames carry the templates that matter.
- The final call footer was a stripped down one off. It now carries the same
  signature block as everything else, because a shorter email should not read as
  a different sender.
- Sponsor slots became white cards with a hairline edge, and the gallery button
  got its missing bottom padding.
- Added the split correspondence template and a filled preview of all twelve.

Then two corrections from CCC's own printed collateral, which is more accurate
than the announcement emails:

- The Confluence speaker card gives **Stonemont Financial**, not Stonemont, and
  confirms Marc A. Hershberg as the seventh speaker rather than only a moderator.
- The sponsor sheet lists **fifteen platinum partners**, not the six the AI
  Revolution email named, and about forty gold. Both lists in `fill-mock.mjs` now
  come off that sheet. The templates still render six platinum slots, so the
  sponsor email needs its grid expanded before it can carry all fifteen.

All twenty five HTML files pass tag balance. Every asset URL returns 200 and the
served bytes match the local files.
